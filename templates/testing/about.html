ABOUT NUTRIFARM AI

Helping farmers make sense of feed quality.

NutriFarm AI is a college project exploring how a simple digital platform can make feed and silage quality information easier for dairy farmers to organize and understand.

The goal is to support informed conversations and everyday decisions with clear sample records, understandable results, and practical guidance. This project is being developed one useful step at a time.

🌱

Farmer-first by design

We want useful feed information to be clear and accessible, while keeping the experience straightforward for dairy farmers.

NutriFarm AI is currently a project foundation. Sample testing and assessment capabilities are planned for later phases.
